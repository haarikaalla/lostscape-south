# Headline Findings Atlas

This atlas is computed from files in `results/`. It reports negative and unavailable findings; it is not a manuscript and makes no discovery claim.

## Cross-state findings

- The calibrated PU model did not beat the best baseline.
- Leakage-safe 225-km conformal evaluation missed its nominal coverage target.
- Terrain was the strongest coarse ablation group; SAR added no demonstrated coarse-scale value.
- Hampi showed modest recovery under a 75-km withheld buffer.
- Landsat pilot spectral trends do not identify individual heritage loss.
- No zone currently has two genuinely independent evidence layers; all showcased convergence zones are Evidence Level 1.

## State profiles

- [Karnataka](karnataka.md): Sparse-label result: the withheld Sannathi target ranked highly, but area-wide performance did not exceed random.
- [Andhra Pradesh](andhra_pradesh.md): The broader withheld-site model beat random on area-wide ranking, while the named target cell itself ranked only moderately.
- [Tamil Nadu](tamil_nadu.md): The strongest primary-label coverage produced the best state-transfer baseline, but the Mandapakkadu blind target was weak.
- [Kerala](kerala.md): Insufficient data: only eight coarse primary-positive cells support transfer evaluation and no settlement-mound blind test is available.
