# Real-world impact and claim ladder

## Decisions the current system can support

1. Select generalized cells for archaeologist-led desk review while balancing archaeological score, uncertainty and modeled documentation propensity.
2. Compare survey strategies at fixed area budgets instead of relying on accuracy in a rare-positive setting.
3. Prioritize known-site regions for construction-pressure inspection, while showing backtest weakness and intervals.
4. Identify where new systematic surveys would most improve cross-state evidence, especially outside Tamil Nadu.
5. Share reproducible maps without publicly releasing precise vulnerable coordinates.

## Claims supported now

- Open-source inventory coverage is highly uneven across states and classes.
- A coarse full model retrospectively ranks dense withheld clusters better than random in PR-AUC.
- Hampi/Vijayanagara recovery is modest under a spatial buffer.
- The current PU implementation does not beat the strongest baseline.
- Exact five-year construction-change magnitude has no demonstrated skill beyond persistence.
- Documentation-propensity correction is weak but produces seed-stable top-50 lists.

## Claims requiring additional evidence

| Desired claim | Minimum additional evidence |
|---|---|
| Candidate is an archaeological site | Independent expert review and permitted field verification |
| Candidate is a settlement or city | Verified spatial extent, diagnostic material/structures, chronology and settlement-function evidence |
| Candidate belongs to an empire/culture | Secure dating and culturally diagnostic archaeological evidence |
| Site will be damaged | Longitudinal verified damage labels plus planning/quarry data and successful prospective backtesting |
| Quarry threat forecast | Validated quarry classifier with the required visual sample and temporal expansion labels |
| Ancient water-system prediction | Substantially more verified tank/canal labels and a separate leakage-safe evaluation |
| Generalizes across South India | Balanced independent labels and prospective surveys in every state/ecozone |

## What would materially strengthen a high-impact remote-sensing study

- Fine 10-m optical/SAR/terrain patches and a CNN or foundation-embedding comparison under identical spatial splits.
- Independent expert review of blinded candidate and control chips.
- Prospective field survey with measured coverage and detection probability.
- Historical CORONA/map features that were never used for training.
- Road, settlement, hydrology, lithology and protected-area layers with documented licenses.
- A validated quarry-growth layer and site-footprint damage trajectories.
- External replication in a fifth region and a preregistered prospective holdout.

These additions would strengthen the evidence; they must not be described as completed until their result files exist.
