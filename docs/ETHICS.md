# Ethics and site protection

Archaeological coordinates can enable looting, trespass, vandalism, and unregulated collection. LostScape-South therefore releases only generalized 5–10 km cells for unprotected sites and keeps exact coordinates in gitignored private storage.

Predictions are candidates for professional field verification. They must not be described as discoveries, used to advertise vulnerable places, or treated as evidence for enforcement, ownership, development approval, or legal protection status. Archaeologists, local communities, landholders, and relevant heritage authorities should be engaged before field activity or release of sensitive findings.

Survey and documentation bias can reproduce unequal research attention. Accessibility correction is reported alongside uncorrected rankings; uncertainty and sparse-state limitations remain visible. Expert review results must come from real reviewers and are never simulated.
